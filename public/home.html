The

MODEL

FRAME is an applied model of human motivation—a tool for navigating work, life, and relationships with greater clarity and insight. It looks beyond behavior, skills, and strengths to reveal the underlying architecture shaping how you experience the world.

The

APPROACH

Built for a broad range of contexts, FRAME can be implemented one-on-one and at scale. It has been relied on for nearly a decade in moments that demand more than instinct: what to choose, who to trust, how to lead, when to change, why groups succeed—or don’t—and where to aim when the path is unclear.

The

PROCESS

The work begins by mapping the core factors that drive how you perceive situations, make decisions, confront obstacles, and engage with others. From there, the focus shifts to real-world application—distilling the findings into a practical resource for addressing the choices, challenges, and priorities that matter most.

The

OUTCOME

FRAME is an adaptive system that evolves alongside the individuals and organizations it serves. Engineered with the option of native AI integration, it extends beyond the limits of a fixed model, becoming increasingly valuable as circumstances shift, roles transform, and new technologies emerge.
